# Game Description

*Set in the near future, where AI is incorporated into people lives, the main character is trying to navigate in the complex world of hackers,
corporations, AI, and love. *

## Story

The game opens in the near future, where the protagonist works as a software developer for one of the world’s leading AI corporations.

The main objective is to uncover the truth behind the protagonist’s existence: Is their life real, a sophisticated simulation, or merely a spark of consciousness within an AI system?

As the story unfolds, the game explores questions of sentience, consciousness, artificial intelligence, and the nature of existence. It challenges the player to question where the boundary between virtuality and reality truly lies.
